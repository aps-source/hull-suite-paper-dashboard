Strategy rules

Hull Suite (InSilico): the Hull line is GREEN when it is above its value 2 bars ago, RED otherwise. A flip to green = go LONG, a flip to red = go SHORT; the opposite flip closes the trade and reverses. Signal on the closed 15-min bar, fill at the next bar's open. Positional: trades are held overnight, always in the market after the first flip. HMA = weighted Hull average, EHMA = the same built from exponential averages. Nothing else — no CPR, no EMA exit (both tested and rejected). 1 lot per strategy.
